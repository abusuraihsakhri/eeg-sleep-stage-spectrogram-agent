EEG Spectrogram Laboratory

Single-channel spectral exploration and deterministic metric auditing

Runs entirely in your browser

Research and educational use only. This application computes power spectral density, not AASM sleep-stage labels or clinical diagnoses. Uploaded samples stay on your device; there is no backend connection. Avoid uploading identifiable patient data.

EEG input

One amplitude column (µV), optionally preceded by a time column. A header row is allowed. Maximum 250,000 samples / 5 MB.

Legacy metric audit

Preserves the Python backend's example rules. These arbitrary cutoffs are not clinically validated EEG criteria.

Select “Run local audit” to evaluate metric thresholds.

Time–frequency spectrogram

Hann-windowed FFT with 50% overlap. One-sided power spectral density in µV²/Hz; colors show relative log power. No filtering, artefact correction, or automatic sleep-stage classification.
